Who Is Glenn Close?

Glenn Close is an American actress and singer who has built an impressive career in Hollywood and on Broadway. She is widely recognized for playing complex, powerful, and memorable characters.

She was born on March 19, 1947, in Greenwich, Connecticut, United States. Before becoming a major Hollywood star, Close developed her acting skills through theater performances.

Her breakthrough film appearance came in The World According to Garp (1982), where she starred alongside Robin Williams. Her performance earned her an Academy Award nomination and introduced her to a much wider audience.

Over the years, she has appeared in numerous successful films and television productions, establishing herself as one of the most versatile performers in the entertainment industry.

Glenn Close Movies That Made Her Famous

Glenn Close has appeared in many memorable movies throughout her career. Some of her performances have become particularly popular with audiences and critics.

1. Fatal Attraction (1987)

Fatal Attraction is one of the most recognizable films in Glenn Close's career. She played Alex Forrest in this psychological thriller, delivering a performance that became a major talking point in Hollywood.

The film received significant attention and earned Close an Academy Award nomination for Best Actress.

2. Dangerous Liaisons (1988)

In Dangerous Liaisons, Close portrayed the Marquise de Merteuil. Her performance demonstrated her ability to play sophisticated and emotionally complex characters.

The movie earned her another Academy Award nomination and further strengthened her reputation as a talented dramatic actress.

3. 101 Dalmatians (1996)

Glenn Close became internationally recognizable to another generation of movie fans through her portrayal of Cruella de Vil in Disney's live-action 101 Dalmatians.

Her distinctive interpretation of the character made the movie one of her most memorable family-film appearances.

4. 102 Dalmatians (2000)

Close returned as Cruella de Vil in the sequel 102 Dalmatians. Her energetic performance helped bring the iconic Disney villain back to the screen.

5. Albert Nobbs (2011)

In Albert Nobbs, Close played the title character in a historical drama. She also contributed to the screenplay and production.

Her work on the film earned another Academy Award nomination and demonstrated her creative involvement beyond acting.

6. The Wife (2018)

The Wife is another important movie in Glenn Close's filmography. She portrayed Joan Castleman, a woman dealing with complicated personal and professional circumstances.

Her performance received widespread critical recognition and earned her a Golden Globe Award for Best Actress in a Motion Picture – Drama.
